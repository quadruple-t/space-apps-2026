Do

Load Archivo with the wdth axis. Set headlines in sentence case and uppercase with CSS, so screen readers hear normal text.

Don't

Don't substitute Inter, Montserrat, or a condensed grotesque. Don't add tracking to display type. Don't set paragraphs in mono.

03Logo

The sun on
the ridge.

The mark is a lunar disc cut by a ridgeline, with the Sun resting on the horizon — the exact moment of grazing light the product exists to show.

Shadowline

Clear space

Half the disc diameter on every side — 14 px at the default 28 px mark. Nothing enters this zone.

Minimum size

Mark alone 24 px. Full lockup 120 px wide.

Color

Stroke and ridge in bone. Sun dot always #ff5364. Never place the lockup on coral, blue, or purple.

Don't

Motion rules

Do

One orchestrated entrance per page. Stagger 90–120 ms, four items maximum. Transform and opacity only.

Do

Don't

No bounce, spin, or parallax for its own sake. No animated gradients. No auto-playing motion on social without a static version.

05Voice

Calm flight
director.

Cinematic product, unromantic copy. Short sentences, concrete nouns, and uncertainty stated out loud. The visualization carries the emotion; the words carry the facts.

Say

  • Sun elevation +1.6°, grazing the horizon.
  • Terrain decides which sites keep a line to Earth.
  • Illustrative values.
  • Find the strongest candidate.
  • Launch Freeplay.
  • Lunar south pole.

Not

  • Bathed in ethereal lunar light.
  • Unlock the power of mission intelligence.
  • Sample data.
  • Revolutionary multi-factor scoring engine.
  • Get started today!
  • Moon's South Pole (in body copy).

Copy standards

Sentence case

Store copy in sentence case. Uppercase is a CSS treatment for display type, never typed capitals.

Numbers

One decimal for angles. Degree symbol with a space: +1.6°, 89.5° S, 222.7° E.

Names

Shadowline is one word. Capitalize Freeplay, Guided Mission, and Mission Comparison as product modes.

Buttons

Verb first, two to three words: Launch Freeplay, Begin Tutorial, Explore the Moon, Compare Missions.

Required disclosure

Every public asset showing the simulatorPlex Mono · ≥ 11px · bone/60

Conceptual preview. Procedurally generated terrain and illustrative values, not mission telemetry or LOLA-derived data.

06Marketing applications

One accent,
one idea.

Every asset should feel like a frame from the instrument: mostly void, one accent, a number that means something, and the disclosure in place.

Social

1080 × 1080 / 1350

  • Void background, one 2–5 word headline
  • One accent element only
  • HUD cluster in a corner for texture
  • Logo bottom-left, 120 px lockup
  • Disclosure bottom-right, ≥ 11 px
  • Three text elements maximum
Slides

16:9 presentation

  • 64 px margins, dark throughout
  • Title 64–96 px, body 24–28 px
  • One idea per slide
  • Direct labels over legends
  • Source line in Plex Mono
Print

One-pager

  • Dark page if full color
  • Bone page + void text if constrained
  • 12-column grid, 8 px base unit
  • Display at 80% of screen size
  • Disclaimer and Space Apps line mandatory
Video

Screen capture

  • 1440p+, void, no browser chrome
  • Keep the HUD in frame
  • Slow, single-direction moves
  • Plex Sans captions, mono timestamps
  • End card: logo, tagline, disclaimer

Co-branding

Do

Keep clear space around both the Shadowline lockup and the NASA Space Apps mark. Never merge them into one unit.

Don't

Never imply NASA endorsement, use the NASA meatball as decoration, or omit the non-affiliation line.
